UDSpot Improving Road Safety & Accident Prevention Using Ultrasonic Distance Sensors in Vehicle Detection in Blind Corners

This research developed a prototype road-safety system that detects approaching vehicles on blind corners using Ultrasonic Distance Sensors (UDS), processed by an Arduino UNO R3 and relayed to drivers through RGB LED traffic signals. Built on the ADDIE model and validated through a 1:28-scaled diorama simulation, the prototype avoided vehicle standoff in 6 of 6 scenarios — versus only 2 of 6 without it.

Why this matters

Roads kill people. Blind corners hide the danger.

The World Health Organization (2023) reports about 1.19 million deaths every year from road traffic incidents — more than half of them pedestrians and motorists. In the Philippines, road deaths rose by 39% in a single decade.

The gap: convex mirrors fog and get hidden by vegetation; camera systems fail in fog and low visibility; radar-based solutions are costly. UDSpot attacks the problem with ultrasonic sensors — color- and type-agnostic, rain-tolerant, and affordable.

The answer: detect the vehicle before the corner, and translate that detection into traffic signals drivers already understand — green = clear, yellow = caution, red = stop. Aligned with the PH National Road Safety Action Plan 2023–2028 (50% fatality reduction by 2030).

How it works

Sense → Process → Signal

A vehicle approaches a corner that neither driver can see. The system senses it, decides who goes first, and tells both drivers — before they ever meet.

01

Sense

Four Ultrasonic Distance Sensors emit high-frequency sound waves and time the echo. Any vehicle — any color, any type — within range is detected. Rain barely affects sound.

02

Process

An Arduino UNO R3 runs two C++ circuits. Circuit 1 guides the vehicle exiting the one-lane road; Circuit 2 runs the anti-standoff logic inside it — first to trigger, first to go.

03

Signal

Five RGB LEDs speak the language of traffic: solid green, flashing yellow, solid red, flashing red — each state computed from live sensor input in real time.

04

Prevent

Standoffs at the blind corner simply don't form: 6/6 scenarios resolved in the simulation, versus 2/6 without the system. The alternative hypothesis was accepted.

Headline results

Three research questions, three clean verdicts

Blind corner — without UDSpot

2 / 6 scenarios avoided

Drivers rely on vision alone. Vehicles approaching around the corner remain invisible, and standoffs occurred in 4 of 6 scenarios — the two vehicles simply could not see each other.

Blind corner — with UDSpot

6 / 6 scenarios avoided

Every scenario resolved without a standoff. The system detected traffic, sequenced the signals, and communicated priority to both sides — proof of efficacy in the simulated environment.
