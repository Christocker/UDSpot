Finding 4 · The decisive test

Vehicle standoff: 6/6 with UDSpot, 2/6 without

Two vehicles met at the blind corner in six scenarios — once with the system and once without (Tables 5–7). Without UDSpot, standoffs occurred in four of six scenarios; with UDSpot, none.

H₀ · Null hypothesis

“UDSpot is not significantly effective when tested in a simulated environment.”

→
Hₐ · Alternative hypothesis

“UDSpot is significantly effective when tested in a simulated environment.”

Verdict: the UDSpot system was successful in avoiding vehicle standoff between two vehicles in a simulated environment. The alternative hypothesis was accepted and the null hypothesis was rejected.
